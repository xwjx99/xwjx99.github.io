Welcome

I study artificial intelligence, social media analytics, and computational methods for public health.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Software and Information Systems at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. My research focuses on natural langugae processing, large language models, social media intelligence, and public health informatics.

My work develops scalable and interpretable AI methods for understanding large collections of short, noisy, and rapidly changing online discussions.

Biography

About Me

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, information systems, natural language processing, and public health. I am particularly interested in designing computational methods that make social media data more useful for scientific analysis and public decision-making.

My current projects investigate LLM for social media analytics, natural language processing and text mining, AI for public health applications, and trustworthy & advanced topic modeling.

Current Work

Research

LLM-Enhanced Topic Modeling

Developing model-agnostic approaches that use large language models to improve semantic representation and topic quality for short social media texts.

Public Health Analytics

Studying public concerns, opinions, sentiment, and information behavior using large-scale social media data.

Temporal Discourse Analysis

Modeling how online topics emerge, persist, disappear, and transform across different time periods.
